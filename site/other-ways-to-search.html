Other ways to search your own files

Trawl by Crane AI Labs is one of several tools that search files you own. Here are some others, each with a link to what its makers say. It is a list, not a ranking.

How to read this page

We read each source page on 21 October 2026 and kept to what it says. Where a page did not say something, we left it out. These products change, so follow the link for the current picture.

We left out products whose details we could not check against the maker’s own page.

The list

DigUp

A computer app

  • Its page says it searches “the files on your computer by what’s in them, with Google DeepMind’s EmbeddingGemma 2”: the same family of model Trawl uses.
  • It lists pictures, PDFs, documents, audio, video and, if you opt in, source code.
  • When we checked, the Mac version was stable and the Windows and Linux versions were in beta. The page lists no phone app.
  • MIT licence. It says it goes online only to download the model once and to check for updates once a day.

github.com/ARahim3/DigUp · checked 21 October 2026

Files by Google: Smart Search

An Android app

  • Google’s help page says it finds files using text from images and PDFs, location and objects from images, and the artist, album and title of audio and video.
  • It says “Scanning happens on your device so your information doesn’t leave your device.”

support.google.com/files/answer/9765316 · checked 21 October 2026

TIDY

An Android app, for photos

  • Its page says it works entirely offline and searches photos from text and from another photo, using the OpenCLIP model.
  • On Google Play and F-Droid. GPL-3.0 licence.

github.com/slavabarkov/tidy · checked 21 October 2026

PicQuery

An app for Android, iOS and computers, for photos

  • Its page says “Photos, indexes, and model inference stay on your device.”
  • Runs on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux. MIT licence. It lists images only.
  • On GitHub Releases. Its page says the Play Store version has not yet been updated.

github.com/greyovo/PicQuery · checked 21 October 2026

Ente

A photo storage service with search on the device

  • Its page says the search runs on the device, and that the search indexes are “stored locally. They also get encrypted and stored on the server.”

ente.com/ml · checked 21 October 2026

Immich

A photo server you run yourself

  • Its documentation says the smart search runs on the server you host, under Administration, Settings, Machine Learning, not on the phone.

docs.immich.app/features/smart-search · checked 21 October 2026

Google Photos: Ask Photos

A feature in the Google Photos app

  • Google’s help page says “You can only use Ask Photos with images that are backed up.”
  • It also says the feature isn’t available in all regions, and that you have to opt in. It describes the feature as experimental.

support.google.com/photos/answer/15318661 · checked 21 October 2026

What Trawl does

Trawl searches photos, screenshots (including the words on them), documents, videos and voice notes in one search, on an Android phone. The app has no internet permission and needs no account. Here is how to check that.

If you only need to search photos, several tools above list photo search. If you want to search a computer’s files, DigUp does. Trawl’s own pages say what Trawl does: What Trawl is.
