Fact sheet

Name
Trawl. Write Trawl by Crane AI Labs on first mention.
Made by
Crane AI Labs
What it does
Finds photos, screenshots, documents, videos and voice notes on an Android phone by what is in them, from words you type.
Works on
Android 11 or newer. Trawl for 64-bit phones, Trawl Go for 32-bit Android Go phones.
Size
Trawl: the app is about 47 MB, and the models are 670 MB (Standard) or 304 MB (Save data). Trawl Go: about 21 MB, with no model download. Trawl Go finds files by their names and the words in them; it does not search by meaning or listen to recordings.
Privacy
No internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics. How to check it.
Model
EmbeddingGemma 2 by Google (Apache 2.0), running on the phone.
Languages
English works best today. Luganda and Swahili are being measured, not claimed.
Price
Free.
Licence
Open source under Apache 2.0. Source code on GitHub.
Version
1.0.4, released 30 October 2026 (1.0.0 and 1.0.1 on 22 October, 1.0.2 and 1.0.3 on 23 October). Download.
Computers
Windows, Mac and Linux: a developer preview with unsigned installers, released 22 October 2026. Details.
Website
trawl.craneailabs.com
