How the files were made:
- **Stills:** single frames from the 1080p originals, cropped and sized to twice their largest display size. Each is saved as
  AVIF, with a WebP fallback.
- **The clip:** 6 seconds of the 1080p original, with no sound, as VP9 WebM at 1280×720 and 854×480, plus H.264 MP4 at
  854×480 for older browsers.
- **Drawn parts:** the screenshot, the receipt, the chat and the voice note are drawn in HTML. Their names, numbers and
  messages are made up.
