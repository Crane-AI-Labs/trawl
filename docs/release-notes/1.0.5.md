- **Keeps reading in the background.** Android was stopping Trawl for using too much power while it worked unseen. Trawl now does
  its heavy work only while Android protects it (with its notification, or as a scheduled job), so it no longer gets stopped. When
  you plug in, a stopped Trawl starts again by itself. Below 90 % battery Android itself waits about 15 minutes before it counts the
  phone as charging.
- **Finds words shown in videos.** Words on screen in a video (a caption, a sign, a slide) are now found at the moment they appear,
  and one-word captions are found too. Ordinary camera videos cost no extra time to read.
- **Comes back the way you left it.** On Samsung phones, returning to Trawl could bring the keyboard back by itself and jump the
  results into the typing layout. It now comes back as you left it.
- **Clearing a search is clean.** Tapping the X no longer flashes the filter buttons over your photos.
- **Large text and small screens.** The selection bar, filter chips, home cards, document rows and add-on titles now fit at large
  text sizes, on small phones and with the phone on its side.
- **Steadier.** Tapping Back or Open twice quickly no longer opens two screens or two "Open with" boxes. Turning the phone keeps
  your place in the list and in setup. A file that is broken or huge no longer keeps Trawl busy forever. A full phone now says so.
- **Easier with a screen reader.** Share buttons say which file they share, and result rows say how old the file is.

Already on 1.0.0 to 1.0.4? Install 1.0.5 over it. It has the same signing key, so your index and settings stay.

Trawl never changes, moves or uploads your files. It has no internet permission at all.
