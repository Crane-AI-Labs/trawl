The first release. Trawl finds things on your phone by what's in them, not by file name:

- **Photos and screenshots** by what they show ("people in graduation gowns", "the bus timetable"), including the words in them.
- **Documents** by what they say: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, ePub, CSV, RTF and text.
- **Videos** by what's on screen, and **voice notes and recordings** by their sound, plus the words in English speech.
- **Find similar**: from any photo, the photos that look like it.
- Search in English, Luganda, Swahili, Runyankole, Acholi, Ateso, Lugbara, Kinyarwanda and more. English works best today;
  the others are getting better.
- **Private blur** for photos that may be sensitive, and **quick look**, which makes every photo searchable soon after install.
- Polite in the background: while you use another app, Trawl's heavy work waits. It works while Trawl is open, or while the
  phone is put down, and it saves the slow work for the charger.

Trawl never changes, moves or uploads your files. It has no internet permission at all.
