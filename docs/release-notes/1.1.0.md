- **Older Excel files are read.** Spreadsheets saved in the old Excel format (.xls, from Excel 97 to 2003) used to be found only by
  their name. Trawl now reads the words and numbers inside them, the same way it already reads .xlsx files. After the update, Trawl
  reads only your .xls files again; nothing else is read twice. It applies to Trawl and Trawl Go.

- **Numbers in a search match the way they are written in files.** A search for "50k" or "50,000" now also finds a file that says
  50000, so prices, totals and amounts in spreadsheets and statements are easier to find.

- **Documents you sent on WhatsApp and Telegram are found.** WhatsApp and Telegram keep the documents you send in folders that ask
  other apps not to look inside, so Trawl skipped them. Trawl now reads the documents and PDFs in those folders, but still stays
  out of their private folders, and out of the chat databases and backups.

- **Songs and singing in videos and voice recordings are understood better.** Trawl now listens to a sung stretch as a whole and
  judges sung words on their own, so the words of a song are more often kept and found. A search such as "man singing" or "woman
  singing" now also finds a video where Trawl heard a man or a woman singing. Videos and recordings with singing that Trawl had
  already read get the new reading of their sound only, during full indexing (usually while charging); their pictures are not read
  again. It applies to Trawl.

- **Trawl stays calm while it opens its index.** If Trawl cannot open its index right away (another task is using it, or the phone's
  storage is full), it now says "Opening your index" or that it is retrying, keeps trying on its own, and offers "Try again".
  Searches answer straight away in the meantime, and your results appear as soon as the index is ready.

- **A file that stopped Trawl twice is no longer called too large for the phone's memory** unless the phone really ran short of
  memory. Otherwise Trawl says it could not finish the file, and tries it once more after the next update.

- **"OK" on "The models are already installed." stays dismissed.** Tapping OK could bring the same message back while the models
  file was still in your Downloads folder. It now stays away until a different file arrives.

Already on 1.0.0 to 1.0.9? Install 1.1.0 over it. It has the same signing key, so your index and settings stay.

Trawl never changes, moves or uploads your files. It has no internet permission at all.
