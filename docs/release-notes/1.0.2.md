- **Your newest files are ready first.** Trawl now reads today's files before older ones, and quick-to-read screenshots and
  documents before photos. On a phone that already holds an older library, the files you got this week are ready to search
  several times sooner. On a Galaxy A06 in our test, the newest 44 files were ready in under 5 minutes instead of over 18.
- **New files show up straight away.** A document that arrives while Trawl is busy reading your library can be found by its
  words about a second after you open Trawl.

Already on 1.0.0 or 1.0.1? Install 1.0.2 over it. It has the same signing key, so your index and settings stay.

Trawl never changes, moves or uploads your files. It has no internet permission at all.
