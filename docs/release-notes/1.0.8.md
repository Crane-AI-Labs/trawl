- **No more "Get the search models" flash when you open Trawl.** On some phones, especially after Android had closed Trawl in the
  background to free memory, opening it briefly showed the models card although the models were installed. Trawl now checks first,
  and opens straight to your home screen.
- **New photos finish their privacy check sooner.** Trawl blurs photos that look private, and shows a photo it has not checked yet as
  blurred with "Checking". Once your photos are searchable, Trawl now finishes those checks at full speed, so on a phone like the Galaxy
  A06 a big new batch of 1,000 photos is fully checked in about 25 minutes instead of over an hour. Searching works just as soon as before.

Already on 1.0.0 to 1.0.7? Install 1.0.8 over it. It has the same signing key, so your index and settings stay.

Trawl never changes, moves or uploads your files. It has no internet permission at all.
