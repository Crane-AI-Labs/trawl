- **Video matches show the right moment.** When a search finds something in a video, the time on the result is now the time
  of the picture Trawl actually matched, so it lands in the right scene. Before, it could be several seconds off and show a
  different scene. Words spoken in videos and voice notes now point at the word itself, usually within a fraction of a second. This applies to videos and voice notes Trawl reads from now on: the ones
  already in your index keep their old times for now.
- **See around the match before you open it.** A video result now shows a row of up to five pictures from just before and
  after the matched moment, with the matched one marked. Tap a picture to open the video there. In the photo grid, tap a
  video's time to see the same row. The pictures are made on your phone, kept in memory only, and blurred when private
  previews are on.
- **Opens at the moment.** Tapping a video result opens it about a second before the match in players that accept a start
  time (VLC, MX Player, mpv, Just Player). Gallery and Google Photos open it from the start.
- **No more "damaged" while the models are still downloading.** If you download the search models in your browser, Trawl
  now waits until the download has really finished before installing it. A download that stopped part-way says how much
  arrived and how to start it again, instead of calling the file damaged. A full phone says so.
- **Faster status counts.** The counts behind the progress meter read a small index, so they stay quick on phones with a
  lot of files.

Already on 1.0.0 to 1.0.3? Install 1.0.4 over it. It has the same signing key, so your index and settings stay.

Trawl never changes, moves or uploads your files. It has no internet permission at all.
