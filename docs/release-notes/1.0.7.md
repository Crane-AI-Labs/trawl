- **Search in Luganda or Swahili, find English files.** Type "lisiiti ya ddwaliro" and Trawl also looks
  for "receipt" and "hospital", so it finds the English receipt, document, screenshot or meme you mean about twice as often as
  before. It works from a small built-in word list (about 120 KB, on the phone, no internet). Small grammar words such as "mu",
  "ku", "nga" and "kwenye" no longer pull in the wrong files, words for "picture" and "message" point at your photos and
  screenshots, and a Luganda word also finds its other forms ("ekifaananyi" finds "ebifaananyi"). English results are unchanged.
- **Your older videos catch up by themselves.** Videos Trawl read before 1.0.5 now get the words shown on screen, and their
  moments point at the right picture, without deleting anything. It happens while the phone charges and takes a second or two per
  minute of video.
- **Tidier at large text and on tablets.** The progress line wraps instead of cutting off, settings values sit under their labels,
  the models note stays one line while you search, long file names keep their ending, and the video strip no longer jumps after it
  appears.
- **Clearer while it waits.** When Trawl pauses because you are using another app, its notification now says so.
- **Runyankole, Kinyarwanda and Acholi searches find English files.** Like Luganda and Swahili (above), Trawl recognises a
  search in these languages and also looks for the English words it means, from a small built-in word list (on the phone, no
  internet). In tests, the right file came first about half the time in Runyankole and Kinyarwanda, up from about a third. English,
  Luganda and Swahili results are unchanged.
- **Photos are blurred until Trawl has checked them.** Trawl blurs photos that look private. Until now, a photo it had not checked
  yet (for example right after you install it, or a photo taken on battery) was shown clear. Now it shows blurred with "Checking",
  is checked straight away while it is on screen, and clears in a second or two if it is fine. Tap it to see it at once.

Already on 1.0.0 to 1.0.5? Install 1.0.7 over it. It has the same signing key, so your index and settings stay.
(1.0.6 was tested but not published; everything in it is in 1.0.7.)

Trawl never changes, moves or uploads your files. It has no internet permission at all.
